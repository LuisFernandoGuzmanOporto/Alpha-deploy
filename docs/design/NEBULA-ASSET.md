## Prompt utilizado

Create an original horizontal 16:9 deep space nebula backdrop for the Earth Analogs science website. Reference is a telescope image with a dense field of fine multicolored pinprick stars, bright white diffraction-star jewels, sapphire-blue voids, luminous ivory wisps of dust, sharply textured amber-orange and golden yellow dust pillars and edges. Capture that magical whimsical sense of vastness and intricate organic filaments, rather than soft blurry gradients or flat colored clouds. Dominant almost-black navy space, rich deep blue, vivid purple nebula pockets, fiery burnt orange and gold illuminated cloud rims, small silver-white luminous ribbons. Composition: left-center and upper left remain mostly dark star field for white UI headings, exquisitely detailed orange/gold turbulent dust along the right edge and lower third, a few violet veils in upper right. Include depth, tiny sparkling stars of diverse sizes but reserve only a few large points of light. No text, no letters, no website UI, no planets, no cartoon, no illustrations of spacecraft. The entire result is a decorative imagined star nursery, not a scientific observation. Landscape wide image suitable as a background; high detail and natural photographic texture.
